# SkyReserve Baggage Policy

## Carry-On Baggage
Every passenger, regardless of fare type, may bring one carry-on bag (max 7kg, max dimensions 55 x 40 x 20 cm) and one small personal item (handbag, laptop bag, or similar) free of charge. Carry-on bags exceeding these limits will be gate-checked at the airline's discretion.

## Checked Baggage Allowance
- **Economy fares:** 20kg checked baggage included, in a single piece not exceeding 32kg.
- **Business fares:** 30kg checked baggage included, across up to two pieces.
- **Infants (under 2 years, no seat purchased):** 10kg checked baggage allowance, plus one collapsible stroller or car seat free of charge.

## Excess Baggage Charges
Baggage exceeding the included allowance is charged at ₹500 per additional kilogram, payable at check-in. Excess baggage charges cannot be added retroactively after departure.

## Restricted and Prohibited Items
Prohibited in both carry-on and checked baggage: flammable liquids, compressed gases, explosives, and corrosive substances. Lithium battery power banks above 100Wh require airline approval and must be carried in cabin baggage only, never checked. Sharp objects (knives, scissors over 6cm) must be packed in checked baggage only.

## Damaged or Delayed Baggage
Passengers must report damaged or missing baggage to airport ground staff within 24 hours of arrival. SkyReserve is not liable for damage to items that are fragile, perishable, or not properly packed. Delayed baggage claims are tracked via a Property Irregularity Report (PIR) issued at the airport counter.

## Sports and Special Equipment
Sports equipment (skis, golf bags, surfboards) can be carried as checked baggage for an additional fee of ₹1,500 per item, subject to space availability. Musical instruments too large for the overhead bin may be purchased an extra seat in advance.
