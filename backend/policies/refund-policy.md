# SkyReserve Refund Policy

## Refund Timelines
Approved refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to the original payment method. UPI and wallet refunds are typically fastest (1-3 business days); credit card refunds can take up to 10 business days depending on the issuing bank.

## What Gets Refunded
Refunds are calculated on the base fare and applicable taxes only. Convenience fees, payment gateway charges, and any add-on services already consumed (such as seat selection fees for a seat already flown) are non-refundable.

## Refunds for Cancelled Bookings
Refund amount for a cancelled booking follows the Cancellation Policy's fee schedule. The refunded amount is the ticket fare minus the applicable cancellation fee for that timing window.

## Refunds for Failed or Duplicate Payments
If a payment is deducted but the booking fails to confirm (e.g. due to a seat lock expiring or a payment gateway error), the amount is automatically refunded within 5-7 business days. Duplicate payments for the same booking, if they occur, are automatically detected and refunded — no support ticket is required.

## Refund Status Tracking
Refund status can be checked in the "My Bookings" section against the relevant booking. Refunds move through three states: Initiated, Processing, and Completed. Passengers are notified by email at each stage.

## Chargebacks and Disputes
Passengers are asked to wait for the standard refund timeline before filing a chargeback with their bank, since a chargeback filed while a refund is already processing can delay resolution. If a refund has not arrived after 10 business days, passengers should contact SkyReserve support before escalating to their bank.

## Non-Refundable Situations
No refund is issued for a "no-show" (passenger does not check in and does not cancel in advance), for non-cancellable fares, or for bookings cancelled after the flight's scheduled departure time.
