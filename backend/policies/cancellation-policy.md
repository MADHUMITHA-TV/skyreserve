# SkyReserve Cancellation Policy

## Free Cancellation Window
Bookings can be cancelled free of charge within 24 hours of purchase, provided the flight departs more than 7 days from the original booking date. Outside this window, standard cancellation charges apply.

## Cancellation Charges by Timing
- **More than 7 days before departure:** ₹1,000 cancellation fee per passenger, remainder refunded to original payment method.
- **Between 7 days and 24 hours before departure:** 50% of the ticket fare is retained as a cancellation fee.
- **Within 24 hours of departure:** Ticket is non-refundable; only statutory taxes and fees are returned.

## How to Cancel
Cancellations can be made through the "My Bookings" section of the SkyReserve app or website, or by contacting customer support. A booking can only be cancelled by the account that made it, or by an administrator. Once a booking is cancelled, the seat is released back into inventory immediately.

## Cancellations Due to Airline-Initiated Changes
If SkyReserve cancels or reschedules a flight by more than 3 hours, passengers may cancel free of charge and receive a full refund, or rebook on an alternative flight at no additional cost, regardless of how close to departure the change occurs.

## Partial Cancellations on Multi-Passenger Bookings
Each passenger on a booking can be cancelled independently; cancellation fees are calculated per passenger, not per booking. Cancelling all passengers on a booking cancels the booking itself.

## Non-Cancellable Fares
Certain promotional or discounted fares may be marked non-cancellable at the time of booking. These fares are excluded from the free 24-hour cancellation window and cannot be refunded under any circumstances except an airline-initiated change.
